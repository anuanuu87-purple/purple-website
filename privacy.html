Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

1. Local-First Architecture

PURPL3 OS AI by 1into1 runs locally on your macOS device. 1into1 operates zero proxy servers to record, collect, or store your voice audio, local documents, directory contents, or synthesized reports.

2. Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Processing

Voice transcription (Whisper) and task reasoning route directly from your machine to Groq endpoints using your private API key, governed by Groq's privacy policies.

3. System Permissions

Accessibility, Microphone, and Automation permissions are requested solely to execute actions you verbally direct on your Mac. No system or audio data is exfiltrated.

4. Payment Processing

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Lemon Squeezy acting as Merchant of Record. 1into1 does not store payment card information.
